## Measurement

- Pixel events already on site: `ViewContent`, `InitiateCheckout`, `Purchase`.
- Create a custom conversion: `Purchase` where the booking used Klarna (tag from the payment provider field) to see Klarna-attributed revenue.
- Watch: CTR > 1%, CPC < $1.20, and checkout completion rate for Klarna vs card.
- Success signal in 14 days: 10%+ of completed checkouts choose Klarna, and average booking value on Klarna is higher than card.
